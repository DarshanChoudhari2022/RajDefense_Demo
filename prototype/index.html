02 Technology

Why our drones run on hydrogen

A hydrogen fuel cell sits between a battery and a petrol engine. It is electric and quiet like the first, and it lasts several times longer.

  1. 01

    Battery buffer for lift

    Vertical take-off needs a short burst of high power. A small battery supplies it, then recharges from the fuel cell in flight.

  2. 02

    Fixed wing for range

    After take-off the aircraft flies on its wing like a plane. That uses far less energy than hovering.

  3. 03

    Fuel cell for cruise

    Hydrogen from an onboard cylinder meets oxygen from the air in a PEM fuel cell. It makes steady electric power for long forward flight.

Battery

Simple, but short

A battery carries limited energy for its weight. With a useful sensor on board, most battery drones fly for an hour or less.

Petrol engine

Longer, but loud

Petrol drones fly longer. They are also noisy, run hot and need regular engine maintenance.

Hydrogen fuel cell

Electric, and lasting

No combustion and no moving engine. The fuel cell makes electricity, water vapour and a little heat, and electric motors do the flying.

Up to 5 hours

What this means in the field

  • Endurance

    Up to 5 hours with a 5 kg payload on the RAJAS RJS-H1.

  • Low signature

    Electric motors and no exhaust flame make it quieter and cooler than an engine-driven drone.

  • Fast turnaround

    A hydrogen cylinder is swapped in minutes. Recharging a large battery pack takes hours.

  • Simple maintenance

    No pistons, carburettor or spark plugs to service.

Every fuel system is leak-tested before it leaves the factory. Ground handling follows the gas-plant safety practice of our parent company.

05 Company

We come from gas engineering

Our parent company, Raj Process Equipments and Systems, has spent years designing plants that compress, clean and store gas at industrial scale. A fuel-cell drone is basically a small, flying gas plant.

Compressed-gas plants hold gas at industrial scale. An aircraft needs the same discipline: a pressure vessel and a regulator.

Plants clean the gas they handle. A fuel cell has a purity requirement of its own.

Every fuel system is leak-tested before it leaves the factory.

A power system that must not fail. Most drone companies have to learn these four skills from scratch.
