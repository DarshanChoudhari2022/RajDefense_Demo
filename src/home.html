Inside the platform

Why we fly
on hydrogen.

Raj’s background in industrial gas engineering led to an aircraft powered by a hydrogen fuel cell. Three elements shape its flight.

01

Fuel-cell cruise

A fuel cell supplies steady electrical power for forward flight. Its outputs include water vapour and heat.

02

Electric lift

A compact battery supports the short bursts of power needed for vertical take-off and landing.

03

Fixed-wing flight

Once airborne, the wing carries the aircraft through cruise, extending its time in the air.
